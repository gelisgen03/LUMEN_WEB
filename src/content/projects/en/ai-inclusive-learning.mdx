## Goal

To give youth workers the skills to bring artificial intelligence into their practice without widening inequalities.

## Activities

1. Research on how young people use AI in each partner country.
2. Co-creation of a curriculum and non-formal education activities.
3. Training of trainers, then local pilots with youth groups.
4. Dissemination events in every country.
