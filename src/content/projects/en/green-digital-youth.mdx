## Goal

To show that digital tools can serve the green transition, provided they are used frugally and critically.

## Activities

1. Workshops on the environmental footprint of digital technology.
2. A creative hackathon: design an online campaign for a local climate cause.
3. Field visits: circular-economy initiatives in Paris.

## Green commitment

Train travel first, local and vegetarian meals, paperless materials.
