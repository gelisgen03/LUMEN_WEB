## Goal

Tackling both the digital divide and electronic waste.

## Activities

1. Collecting equipment from companies and individuals.
2. Repair and refurbishment workshops led by young people.
3. Donations to local families and associations.

## Why it matters

Digital technology accounts for a growing share of global emissions; extending the lifespan of devices is the most effective way to reduce it.
