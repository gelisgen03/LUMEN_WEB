Non-formal education is at the heart of our approach: learning by doing, cooperating and reflecting together.

## Skills we work on

- Digital skills and Industry 4.0.
- Green skills: sufficiency, circular economy, climate.
- Transversal skills: teamwork, intercultural communication, initiative.

Every training produces resources other organisations can reuse.
