European programmes are our main lever for action. We act as coordinator or partner, depending on the project.

## Programmes

- **Erasmus+**: mobility for young people and youth workers, cooperation partnerships.
- **European Solidarity Corps**: volunteering and solidarity projects.
- **CERV**: citizenship, equality, rights and values.
- **Horizon Europe** and **Digital Europe**: research, innovation and digital skills.

We apply the Commission's horizontal priorities: inclusion, digital transformation, environment and participation.
