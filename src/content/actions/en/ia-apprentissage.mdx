Artificial intelligence is changing how we learn, work and get informed. We help young people and professionals understand it, use it and question its limits.

## Our focus

- **AI literacy**: how a model works, why it gets things wrong.
- **AI and pedagogy**: bringing AI into youth work and non-formal education.
- **Ethics**: bias, personal data, the environmental footprint of models.
