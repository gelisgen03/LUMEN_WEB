Digital transformation opens doors, but not for everyone. We work first with young people who have fewer opportunities: NEETs, young people from priority neighbourhoods and rural areas.

## What we do

- Hands-on workshops: online safety, admin procedures, collaborative tools.
- Peer mentoring, where trained young people support the next group.
- Support for small associations modernising their tools.

## Example activity

A four-workshop series, "My first digital portfolio", where every participant leaves with an online CV, a professional email address and secured accounts.
