## In short

- This site sets **no cookies**, so there is no consent banner.
- Audience measurement is anonymous and aggregated (Vercel Analytics, no cookies or persistent identifiers).
- The contact form sends your message by email to the team. **It is not saved** in any database.

## Contact form

Data collected: name, email address, organisation (optional), message.
Purpose: replying to you. Legal basis: your consent.
Recipient: the LUMEN team, via the Resend email service.
Retention: as long as needed to handle your request in our mailbox, and at most 12 months.

## Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data by writing to reseau.numerique.eu@gmail.com. You may also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).
